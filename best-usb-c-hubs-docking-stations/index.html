One cable, full desk

Modern laptops keep shedding ports, which is great for portability and frustrating at a desk where you want a monitor, keyboard, mouse, Ethernet, and power all at once. A USB-C hub or docking station fixes this: you connect a single USB-C cable to your laptop and get back a full array of ports.

The result is that you can dock and undock in one motion. Pull one cable and grab your laptop; plug it back in and your entire desk reconnects.

Hub vs. docking station

The two terms overlap, but there is a useful distinction:

  • A USB-C hub is usually compact and bus-powered (it draws power from your laptop). It is ideal for a single external monitor and a few peripherals, and it travels well. Many include USB-C Power Delivery passthrough so your laptop still charges through the same cable.
  • A docking station is larger, has its own power adapter, and can drive multiple monitors plus many peripherals reliably. Choose a dock if you run a multi-monitor setup or notice a hub struggling to power everything.

Ports that actually matter

Match the ports to what you actually plug in:

  • HDMI or DisplayPort for your external monitor — check the maximum resolution and refresh rate the hub supports, since cheaper units cap out below what your monitor can do.
  • USB-C Power Delivery (PD) passthrough so your laptop charges through the hub. Look for enough wattage to cover your specific laptop.
  • USB-A ports for a keyboard, mouse, and older accessories.
  • Gigabit Ethernet if you want a stable wired connection.
  • SD/microSD slots if you work with cameras.

A note on reliability

Build quality and driver compatibility vary a lot in this category, and a flaky hub causes dropped monitors and disconnects that are maddening to diagnose. Anker is widely regarded as one of the most reliable makers at mainstream prices, with noticeably better consistency than generic no-name units. Whatever brand you choose, favor established names with a track record over the cheapest listing.

Bottom line

Pick a compact bus-powered hub for a single-monitor, travel-friendly setup, or a powered docking station for multi-monitor desks. Confirm the video output matches your monitor and that PD passthrough covers your laptop, and lean toward a reputable brand for reliability.
